# 📊 Power BI Projects

A collection of Power BI dashboards focused on business reporting, sales analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

---

## Retail Sales & Regional Analysis

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing retail sales performance across regions, cities, and product categories. Includes annual sales trends, sales forecasting, client and product breakdowns, and top 5 rankings by city and category.

**Key features:**
- Annual sales trends and year-over-year regional comparison
- Sales forecast modeling
- Client and product breakdown by city, state, and segment
- Top 5 cities and categories by sales volume
